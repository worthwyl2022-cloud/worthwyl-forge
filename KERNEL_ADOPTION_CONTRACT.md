# Kernel Adoption Contract

WorthWyl Studio is subordinate to the Convertible Cranium Kernel.

Only the Kernel may evaluate and issue canonical authority, enforce canonical replay protection, journal canonical state, persist canonical state, issue canonical receipts, or grant authority.

Studio and its retained Forge engine may submit requests, perform bounded local assessment, consume verified receipts, present evidence, and coordinate human work. They may not create a competing authority implementation.

The current governed path includes independent Governance Review Juror One and Governance Review Juror Two review before the governed request reaches the Kernel.

Local fixtures, simulations, browser state, generated reports, screenshots, and historical material remain non-canonical and must be labeled by evidence status.
